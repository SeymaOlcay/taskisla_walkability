# Case Study 1 — Data Literacy in Beşiktaş Square

**Group:** Şeyma Olcay, Merve Civcik, Hamdi Çakır
**Date:** February 2026

## About
This case study examined data literacy, management, and ethics in an architectural context, focused on Beşiktaş Square in Istanbul. Three roles were assigned:
- **Data Engineer** — drone footage processing, data lakehouse architecture -Şeyma Olcay
- **Data Analyst/Scientist** — UAV video → YOLO + DeepSORT for trajectory extraction -Hamdi Çakır
- **Ethics & Compliance Officer** — KVKK and GDPR compliance, privacy by design -Merve Civcik

The problem definition focused on Urban Permeability and Pedestrian Flow Conflict in Beşiktaş Square, where ferry, bus, and metro routes intersect with public and recreational areas.
