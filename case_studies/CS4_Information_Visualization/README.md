## About
Three information visualizations developed from the Wikipedia Italian Architects dataset, each with a distinct narrative.

## The Three Visualizations

### Viz I — How did architecture transform over 500 years?
The transformation of architecture is not a story of individual genius. It is a story of shifting relations — between architects, between building types, between patrons and cities, and eras.

### Viz II — From Local to Global: How Italian Architectural Production Expanded Across Five Centuries
Six architects. Seven stylistic eras. 211 buildings. This data story traces how architectural practice evolved from tightly bound regional programs — Palladian villas in the Veneto, Baroque churches in Rome — toward a globally distributed, institutionally diverse output in the contemporary period.

### Viz III — Does Wikipedia tell the same story everywhere?
A geographic bias analysis of 211 Wikipedia articles on buildings by 8 Italian architects — from Palladio to Renzo Piano. Asks whether buildings located abroad receive more detailed Wikipedia coverage than buildings in Italy.
